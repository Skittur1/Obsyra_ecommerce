## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rare, sensual world of artisanal haute parfumerie. Its character is mysterious, unapologetically luxurious, architectural, and tactile. It bridges the intimacy of private salon consultations with the precision of contemporary digital flagships.

The audience consists of fragrance collectors, high-net-worth connoisseurs, and aesthetic purists seeking sensory indulgence and bespoke curation. The interface evokes quiet reverence, nocturnal intimacy, and artisanal craftsmanship.

The visual direction marries **Nocturnal Minimalism** with **Luminous Glassmorphism**:
- Obsidian, pitch-black canvases mimic flacon glass caught in low light.
- Translucent frosted surfaces float with champagne gold and metallic bronze specular highlights.
- Ambient jewel-toned micro-auras (deep sapphire and radiant ruby) emerge only as atmospheric feedback or olfactory family identifiers.

## Colors

The palette operates under a strict dark-mode-first paradigm, calibrated to avoid pure monochrome dead-space while maintaining obsidian depth.

- **Obsidian Core (Neutral):** Canvas foundation relies on `#050507`, with elevated panels layered over `#0B0C10` and `#13141A`.
- **Champagne Gold (Primary):** `#D4AF37` acts as the focal accent for primary actions, price tags, and focal typography. Supported by `#F3E5AB` for incandescent highlights and `#E5C158` for interactive hover and active states.
- **Metallic Bronze (Secondary):** `#8C6D3B` and `#5E4825` anchor micro-borders, structural dividers, and subheadings, offering warm metallic restraint without visual noise.
- **Atmospheric Jewels (Tertiary & Accents):**
  - **Ruby Glow:** `#9E2A2B` (with soft ambient drop of `#4D1214`) signifies spicy, amber, and oriental olfactory profiles, as well as destructive actions or heart triggers.
  - **Sapphire Sheen:** `#1D2D50` and `#2C4A7F` serve aquatic, woody, and nocturnal fragrance categories, providing quiet depth in selection states.
- **Surface Transparencies:** Frosted glass panels use `rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.72)` paired with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` linear hairline borders.

## Typography

The typographical pairing creates an editorial tension between grand salon traditions and modernist luxury retail.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Display typography draws authority from high-contrast editorial serifs. Titles convey scent narratives, collection monikers, and grand olfactory tiers. Letter-spacing remains tight to neutral on larger sizes, opening up gracefully on smaller headlines.
- **Micro-Copy, Captions & Navigation (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Utilitarian data, formulation notes, and UI labels are set with extra letter-spacing (`0.14em` to `0.22em`) and transformed to uppercase where applicable to evoke engraved packaging and niche perfumery flacon plates.
- **Body Copy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Configured in light and regular weights with generous line height to preserve spacious, air-filled legibility against pitch-black backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an editorial, image-forward fluid grid designed primarily for mobile-first luxury interactions.

- **Mobile (Base < 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Fragrance discovery sequences prioritize full-bleed vertical scrolling, single-column focal items, and edge-to-edge product carousels with visible peek margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates two-column comparative fragrance notes and side-by-side quiz decisions.
- **Desktop / Salon View (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at 1440px with generous `4rem` outer margins to maintain intimate editorial containment.
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` and above) define transitions between olfactory pyramids (Top, Heart, Base), preventing visual claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphic layering and dark metallic halos rather than physical shadows.

1. **Base Surface (Level 0):** Pure obsidian `#050507` depth.
2. **Floated Glass Cards (Level 1):** Background `rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.70)` overlaid with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(160%)`. Outlined by a refined 1px border gradient transitioning from `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` at the top edge to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` at the bottom edge.
3. **Modal & Consultation Drawers (Level 2):** Background `rgba(19, 20, 26, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`. Elevated by an ambient outer glow: `0 20px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`, framed with a persistent 1px `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.20)` metallic rim.
4. **Jewel Halos (State Accents):** Active interactive components cast subtle radial diffuse glows behind cards:
   - *Amber/Woody:* `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12) 0%, transparent 70%)`
   - *Nocturnal/Spicy:* `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(158, 42, 43, 0.15) 0%, transparent 70%)`
   - *Rare Florals/Aquatic:* `radial-gradient(circle, rgba(44, 74, 127, 0.18) 0%, transparent 70%)`

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on architectural geometry inspired by hand-cut crystal flacons and heavy bevel-cut stoppers.

- Corner radii are controlled and soft (`roundedness: 1`), keeping general UI components at `0.25rem` to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Circles are reserved exclusively for note accord wheels, scent droplet indicators, and avatar monograms.
- Fragrance cards and interactive module borders maintain crisp rectangular profiles with microscopic corner rounding to preserve high-end architectural tension.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (The Gold Ingot):** Solid `#D4AF37` fill with dark obsidian text (`#050507`), set in `label-lg` uppercase. Flat metallic finish, transitions to `#F3E5AB` on press. Subtle inner bevel highlight on top edge (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Obsidian Glass):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, text in `#F3E5AB`.
- **Tertiary / Link:** Pure typographic link in champagne gold with an underline spaced 4px below that expands outward from center on interaction.

### Cards & Flacon Showcases
- **Product Card:** Frosted obsidian glass canvas (`rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.7)`), 1px subtle gold perimeter border. Fragrance bottle imagery hovers centered with zero clipping. Price and milliliter volume tags utilize tracked `label-md` typography.
- **Hover/Tap State:** The subtle border increases in brightness to `#D4AF37` at 60% opacity with an underlying scent-family glow.

### Fragrance Quiz & Selection Chips
- **Quiz Option Tile:** Square or portrait ratio tiles containing scent accord imagery or tactile mood textures. Selected state activates an inner champagne gold border and an illuminated ambient backdrop matching the note family (sapphire or ruby).
- **Accord Chips:** Unselected chips feature a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with translucent black fill (`rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.6)`). Selected chips shift to solid dark bronze `#5E4825` with gold typography and a tiny illuminated gemstone-dot indicator.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Text Inputs:** Low-contrast background `#0B0C10` with a fine bottom-only or fully enclosed hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`). Focus states illuminate the border to primary gold with a soft inner gold gradient aura. Placeholder text is rendered in muted slate bronze (`#6E6B65`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square (checkbox) and diamond (radio) geometries. Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`. Checked: Solid `#D4AF37` interior with an obsidian glyph icon.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Olfactory Pyramid Display:** A structured vertical triad representing Top, Heart, and Base notes, bound together by a hairline vertical bronze axis with micro-nodes marking scent longevity in hours.
- **Sillage & Longevity Intensity Bars:** Minimal segmented indicators using slim horizontal champagne gold notches over dark obsidian rails.
